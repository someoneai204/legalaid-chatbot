FAQ

Common questions

Is Legal Mitra a replacement for a lawyer? +
No. Legal Mitra provides general legal guidance and helps you understand your rights. For court proceedings or complex matters, you should engage a qualified lawyer. Our "Connect with a Lawyer" feature connects you directly with verified BCI-registered advocates.
Is my conversation private and confidential? +
Absolutely. All your queries and personal details are fully encrypted and confidential. We never share your information with third parties.
How does the home delivery of legal documents work? +
Once you select your document type and fill in the required details, our lawyers draft the document, get it notarized, and courier it to your address within 2–5 business days. You can also download a digital copy instantly.
Are the lawyers on Legal Mitra verified? +
Yes. Every lawyer on Legal Mitra is verified against the Bar Council of India (BCI) register. We check their enrollment number, practice history, and user reviews before listing them.
Can I use Legal Mitra in Hindi? +
Yes! Legal Mitra fully supports Hindi and English. You can type your question in either language and receive responses in the same language.
